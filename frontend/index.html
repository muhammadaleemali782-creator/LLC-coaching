About Learning Coaching Center (LCC / LLC Varanasi)

Learning Coaching Center (widely recognized as L.C.C. or LLC Coaching) is Varanasi's premier academic coaching institute located in the Education Hub near City Central. We deliver high-yield classroom teaching and digital learning for CBSE and UP Board school students (Classes 1st to 12th), Computer Certification Diplomas (DCA, ADCA, Tally Prime), and Fluent Spoken English training.

Courses & Batches Offered

  • Classes 1st–10th Foundation: Comprehensive coaching in Mathematics, Science, English, and Social Studies with regular DPPs.
  • Classes 11th–12th Science & Commerce: Board Exam and competitive foundations in Physics, Chemistry, Maths, Biology (PCM/PCB) and Accountancy.
  • Computer Diploma Courses: Government-recognized DCA, ADCA, and Tally Prime with 1:1 computer lab practicals.
  • Fluent Spoken English & Communication: Pronunciation, vocabulary, public speaking, and personality development.

Director Mentorship & Student Results

Under the leadership of Director Aman Arora, L.C.C. has maintained a 98.6% board exam pass rate with 1500+ top-ranking students across Varanasi, Kanpur, and Uttar Pradesh.
